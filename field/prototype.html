Where it is enforced

  • 1On the phone, before the form opens The tile is flagged red on the home screen, so the tech knows to carry a second monitor before leaving the van — not after arriving.
  • 2On the phone, at the submit tap The send button stays dead until the second unit, its spacing and a photo of the pair are all filled in. This works with no signal.
  • 3At the API, on upload The server recomputes the set number from the database rather than trusting the phone, and rejects an online submission that owes a duplicate.
  • 4In the database, on deploy A trigger refuses to let a set reach Deployed without its pair. This is the layer that holds against imports, manual updates and future clients.
